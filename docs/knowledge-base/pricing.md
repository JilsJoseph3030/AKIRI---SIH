# How pricing is calculated

Estimated value equals weight in kilograms times the per-kg buying rate for that material category and location. Rates differ by recycler: the match screen shows each authorized recycler's offered rate, distance, and pickup availability. Rates are cached for offline use and refreshed when connectivity returns. Final payout is confirmed at handover after the recycler verifies weight and category.
The recycler dashboard also shows a Market board of indicative wholesale rates with 7-day trends for context — those are market reference numbers, not the payout rate. Final payout is confirmed at handover after the recycler verifies weight and category.
