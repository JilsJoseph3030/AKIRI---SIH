# EPR and e-waste basics

India's E-Waste Management Rules require e-waste to flow to authorized recyclers and producers to meet Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) targets through the CPCB portal. Collectors should hand material only to authorized facilities and keep handover records. Recyclers use their confirmed Akiri transactions as supporting records for their own EPR filing. For exact filing steps and current thresholds, check the CPCB EPR portal or ask the Akiri support channel — rules change and this assistant does not give legal advice.
